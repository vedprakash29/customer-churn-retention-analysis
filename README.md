Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

Project Overview

An end-to-end Data Analytics project focused on analyzing customer churn and retention patterns in a telecommunications company.

The project identifies customer segments with higher churn rates and explores the relationship between customer behavior, service usage, contract types, payment methods, and customer retention.

Project Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn and retention rates.
- Identify customer segments with high churn rates.
- Understand the impact of contract types and payment methods.
- Analyze customer tenure and monthly charges.
- Identify potential revenue exposure due to customer churn.
- Generate actionable business insights.

Tools & Technologies

- Python: Data cleaning, data analysis and data preprocessing.
- Pandas: Data manipulation and analysis.
- MySQL: Data storage and SQL-based analysis.
- Excel: Data reporting and analysis.
- Power BI: Interactive dashboard development.

Project Structure

- "01_Raw_Data" – Original dataset.
- "02_Python" – Python scripts and cleaned dataset.

- "05_Power_BI" – Power BI dashboard.
- "06_Documentation" – Project documentation.

Key Business Findings

- Total Customers: 7,043
- Churned Customers: 1,869
- Retained Customers: 5,174
- Overall Churn Rate: 26.54%
- Overall Retention Rate: 73.46%

Important Insights

- Month-to-month contract customers have a significantly higher churn rate than long-term contract customers.
- Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn.
- Electronic check users show a higher churn rate than other payment-method segments.
- Customers without Online Security and Tech Support show higher churn rates.
- Higher monthly charges are associated with higher customer churn.

Note: These are observed relationships in the dataset and do not establish causation.
