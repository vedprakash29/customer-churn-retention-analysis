Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

1. Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn in a telecommunications company. The goal is to understand customer churn patterns, identify customer segments with high churn rates, and recommend strategies to improve customer retention.

2. Business Problem

Customer churn occurs when customers stop using a company's services. High churn can reduce recurring revenue and increase the need to acquire new customers.

This project uses customer data to investigate churn patterns and identify opportunities to improve customer retention.

3. Business Questions

1. What is the overall customer churn rate?
2. Which customer segments have the highest churn rates?
3. How are contract type, customer tenure, payment method, and internet service related to churn?
4. What monthly charges are associated with churned customers?
5. What actions could help the company improve customer retention?

4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Customers: Total number of unique customers.
- Churned Customers: Number of customers who have left the company.
- Churn Rate: Churned customers divided by total customers, multiplied by 100.
- Average Tenure: Average length of time customers have stayed with the company.
- Monthly Charges of Churned Customers: Total monthly charges associated with customers who have churned.
